A FEW IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS

Questions, answered.

Is this a replacement for a researcher?

No. The platform assists analysis, validation, synthesis and recommendation development. Researchers review the evidence and retain responsibility for the interpretation and decision.

Can I start with a report rather than raw data?

Yes. The validation pathway starts with existing reports. When the underlying data is unavailable, the assessment is limited to the claims and material supplied, rather than an independent statistical replication.

What does “validated” mean?

Validation is an assessment against the evidence available in the engagement. Verdicts distinguish robust support, use with caution, unsupported claims and insufficient information. They do not guarantee that a claim is universally true.

Can recommendations be traced back to findings?

The workflow keeps source findings, intermediate insights, synthesis and recommendations connected. Reviewers can inspect the reasoning and working material behind the final narrative.
