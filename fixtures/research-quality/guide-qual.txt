Discussion guide and approach

Approach
We will hold in-depth interviews with 10 lapsed customers selected purposively to cover different store types and reasons for lapsing. Recruitment will be through the customer database of people who have not visited for six months.

Guide
1. Tell me about the last time you visited one of our stores.
2. What changed in how often you visit?
3. Where do you buy coffee now, and why?

Analysis
Interviews will be transcribed and coded thematically by two researchers who will compare codes.
