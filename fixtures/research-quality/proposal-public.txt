Research proposal: Loyalty programme study

Understanding of the brief
The client wants to understand loyalty among the general public in South Africa and whether loyalty schemes are popular.

Objectives
1. Measure awareness of loyalty schemes among the general public.
2. Identify preferred reward types.
3. Profile heavy coffee drinkers.

Approach
We will run an online survey using an opt-in online panel of South African adults. Respondents will be recruited by the panel provider and screened for coffee consumption in the last month.

Sample
We will achieve 1,000 completes, with quotas on age, gender and province.

Analysis
We will report frequencies and cross-tabulations by region and age, with significance testing at 95%.

Deliverables
A PowerPoint report and data tables.

Timeline
Fieldwork in January 2027, report on 15 March 2027.

Cost
R420,000 excluding VAT.
