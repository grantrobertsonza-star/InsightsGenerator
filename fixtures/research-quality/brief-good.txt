Client brief: Loyalty programme research

Background
Our coffee chain has 120 stores. Repeat visits fell by 8% over the last two quarters while a competitor launched a points app. Our last customer survey was in 2024 and did not cover loyalty schemes.

Business problem
We are losing repeat customers to a competitor's app and do not know whether a loyalty scheme would win them back or only reward people who would come anyway.

Decision
The executive team will decide in March whether to launch a loyalty scheme and which reward type to offer.

Objectives
1. Measure the share of current customers who would join a points-based loyalty scheme.
2. Identify which of three reward types (free drinks, discounts, early access) current customers prefer.
3. Estimate how often members expect to visit compared with their current visits.
4. Understand why lapsed customers stopped visiting.

Methodology requirements
Target population: adult customers who visited at least once in the last 12 months, in South Africa. We expect a quantitative survey of current customers and a small set of interviews with lapsed customers. Results are needed by region (Gauteng, Western Cape, KwaZulu-Natal).

Deadline
Final results are needed by 28 February 2027.

Budget
The budget is between R350,000 and R450,000 including fieldwork.
