## Proposed repair batches (each with its library cases as the regression tests)

- **Batch 55, data reading (L1, L3).** Excel whole numbers beyond 2^53 kept as text in the preview (S1); zero-padded
  identifiers in the flipped layout; .zsav accepted; whole numbers stay whole after a skipped blank row; codes shown as
  `1` not `1.0`; mixed, Mac Roman and BOM-less UTF-16 text refused rather than misread; the in-workbook codebook sheet
  read by the one codebook reader (range cells, D2 to D5); first sheet chosen by respondent shape, not row count;
  resource limit by cells (cases x variables) with the case count checked before the full read, set from measurement
  (a 183-column CSV preview takes about 125 s at 144,090 cases against the deployed 60 s).
- **Batch 56, codebooks and linking (L2).** A codebook entry's identity is its variable name, so a reworded label keeps
  its confirmed link (L052); common answer-list notations read (`1. Yes`, `1: Yes`, `{1, Yes}`, codes on their own rows,
  codes across columns, SPSS "Variable Values" exports) or flagged unread, never silently dropped; more header names
  recognised and title rows skipped; a footer legend ends the table; XLSForm `or_other`; a dictionary split over a
  Variables sheet and a Values sheet merged.
- **Batch 57, tables and claims (L4).** Fractions shown and checked as percentages when the cell format or label says %;
  a stated percentage never compared with a count; the row-label column chosen by content, not position (no more
  collapse to one row); label matching tightened ("55+" no longer matches 18-34); the automatic pattern scan not run
  on aggregated tables (or its results kept as suggestions); the right base for re-derived tests; Word and PDF tables
  across a 5-page reading boundary kept whole, and a failed AI reading reported as failed, not "no tables".
- Already planned: **53** grid questions and question maps (Findex Key and routing, legend rows), **54** Indicator data
  (building), **Findex fixed-width** (.sps + .dat) reader.

## Decisions for Grant (recommended answer first; reply "accept" or change any)

1. **Tables uploads:** read agency banner output as it comes (title, group and column header rows, base rows, several
   tables per sheet). *Recommended: yes* (batch 57 or after).
2. **Fractions with %:** a 0 to 1 value whose cell format or label says % is shown and checked as a percentage, with the
   conversion stated. *Recommended: yes.*
3. **Bases:** significance tests use the unweighted base (or effective base); percentages use weighted. *Recommended:
   yes.*
4. **Pattern scan on aggregated tables:** off. *Recommended: off* (its "Total is an outlier" findings are noise).
5. **"NA" in data:** stays a missing marker, but the import warns when "NA" appears in a column of short codes (it may be
   Namibia). *Recommended: warn.*
6. **Blank text answers:** count as no answer in every format, including SPSS and Stata (today they count as answers
   there). *Recommended: yes, one rule.*
7. **"Not sure":** treated as a non-answer like "Don't know"; a "Don't know which ..." that names its subject stays an
   answer. *Recommended: yes* (your research call).
8. **Range codebook cells** ("1=Very dissatisfied to 5=Very satisfied"): label the two end points. *Recommended: yes.*
9. **Excel header rows:** detect title rows above the header and let the researcher change the header row on the Import
   screen. *Recommended: yes.*
10. **Report tables and chart data:** store a report's own tables so its claims can be checked against them. *Recommended:
    yes, after batch 57* (a feature, not a repair).
11. **Word/PDF sent as Tables:** keep accepting them (and fix their failures). *Recommended: keep.*
12. **"-" and "*" in tables:** "-" read as 0 and "*" as "under 0.5%", stated. *Recommended: yes.*

Settled without asking (stated so you can object): the byte limit is 100 MB (the 20 MB contract text is out of date and
will be corrected); `.sas7bdat` stays accepted as now; IGS-style weights named `w1` are offered as a weight once a linked
codebook calls them one; conflicting duplicate codebook entries are reported, not silently resolved.
